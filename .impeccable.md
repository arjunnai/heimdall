## Design Context

### Users

The in-product user is an on-call SRE investigating a live incident under time pressure. The
portfolio reader is an FDE recruiter or staff engineer who needs to understand the evidence,
control boundary, and engineering depth within 90 seconds.

### Brand Personality

Controlled, forensic, and direct. Heimdall should create confidence without disguising uncertainty
or making autonomy feel magical.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dark, industrial incident-control surface suited to an on-call environment. Dense where evidence
needs comparison, spacious at decisions, with a restrained amber signal color. Avoid generic SaaS
cards, neon AI styling, decorative charts, and chat-bot theater.

### Design Principles

1. Evidence precedes assertion: exact IDs and tool calls stay visible.
2. Human authority is unmistakable at the mutation boundary.
3. Uncertainty and escalation are first-class states, never buried.
4. Operational density should remain scannable, not ornamental.
5. Real and simulated backends must always be labeled honestly.
